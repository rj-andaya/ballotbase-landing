Data & Sourcing / API

The PolicySpan API.

Read-only access to the same evidence layer the product reads: bills, hearings and the spoken record, rulemaking, lobbying and campaign finance connected to policy, published fiscal evidence, and the research corpus of reports, tables and official statistics. Response bodies are identical to what the application reads, so a field you see in the product is the field you get here. Every record carries its source and date.

Base URL https://api.policyspan.ai/api/v1. 26 endpoints, generated from the live OpenAPI specification on 2026-11-02. The machine-readable spec is at /api/v1/openapi.json and the interactive reference at /api/v1/docs.

Authenticate

Keys belong to your organization, not to a person, and are scoped to the jurisdictions in your license. Send the key on every request, either way:

X-API-Key: ps_live_...
Authorization: Bearer ps_live_...

Start with /me. It returns the organization, the key's scopes, the licensed jurisdictions, the per-minute and per-month limits, and month-to-date usage. If this call works, everything else will.

curl -s https://api.policyspan.ai/api/v1/me -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"

The key is shown once at issue time; we store only a hash. If a key leaks, ask for it to be revoked and a new one issued; revocation takes effect within a minute. Jurisdictions today: or (Oregon) and ca (California); GET /jurisdictions lists them with licensed true or false for your key. Keys are issued with a license; ask for one.

Limits and headers

Limits are guardrails on a flat-rate license, not a meter. Every response carries:

X-RateLimit-Limit        requests allowed per minute
X-RateLimit-Remaining    left in the current minute
X-Quota-Remaining        left this month (absent when the key has no ceiling)
X-Request-Id             quote this when reporting a problem

Bulk reads: GET /{jur}/bills returns the whole catalog in one call. Pull it once a day, then poll GET /{jur}/alerts for what moved. Do not crawl bill detail for every bill on a schedule; ask for an export if you need that.

Errors

Errors are JSON with a stable error code and a plain-language message.

StatuserrorMeaning

401api_key_invalidUnknown, revoked, expired, or the organization is not active

429rate_limitedOver the per-minute limit; honor Retry-After
429quota_exceededOver the monthly ceiling; contact us

Examples

# What moved on the Oregon record this week
curl -s "$BASE/or/alerts?days=7" -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"

# One California bill with its history, votes, money and filed positions
curl -s "$BASE/ca/bills/AB/1414" -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"

# Research tied to a bill: documented references first, then topical matches and the state's metrics
curl -s "$BASE/or/bills/HB/2658/research" -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"

# Every official series for a county, with the sites behind the water figures one call away
curl -s "$BASE/research/metrics?geo=Fresno%20County,%20California" -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"
curl -s "$BASE/research/series/ca_dwr_gw_depth_to_water?geo=Fresno%20County,%20California" -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"

# A GAO table, cell for cell, with the page it came from
curl -s "$BASE/research/documents/gao:GAO-27-108496/tables" -H "X-API-Key: $PS_KEY"

Reading the records

  • Positions are filed labels. The record says an organization filed support or is on record opposing. It never says why.
  • Lobbying rows are disclosed filings. They are not evidence of influence, and no spend figure is attached to a single bill unless the filing attaches it.
  • Transcripts are verbatim machine output. Speaker labels are diarization numbers, not names. Confirm wording against the linked video before quoting formally.
  • Research is documented or topical. A document is attached to a bill as documented only when the document names the bill (a CBO cost estimate, an LAO analysis). Everything else is a topical match on the bill's subject, labeled as such.
  • Tables are the publisher's cells. Each carries its caption, units and Source line, a link that opens the PDF at that page, and a verified flag that means every number in the grid was found in the page text. Quote figures from verified tables only. Nothing is computed, rounded or converted.
  • Series state their method. Every value carries the exact source call it came from; shares carry their denominator as universe. ACS and CHAS figures are survey estimates without margins of error.
  • Water series are network roll-ups. A county figure is the median across the wells or gages a network monitors; universe is their count, and the series endpoint lists the sites behind the latest value. Where a state and the federal network both cover a county, both are returned and named.
  • Housing production is by place. Permit rows are permits authorized, not units built; RHNA rows are one per planning cycle; the unincorporated part of a county is its own row. Place ids are the 7-digit state plus FIPS place code.

MCP

The same records are available as tools for Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP hosts, remote at https://mcp.policyspan.ai/mcp with your key as Authorization: Bearer ps_live_.... The tools carry the same names as the endpoint groups above and the same reading rules.
