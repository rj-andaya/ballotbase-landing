What are the published fiscal estimates for California SB 79 (2025–26)? In under 100 words, identify the dated source, summarize one estimate, link the evidence, and flag limitations.

The Senate Appropriations analysis from May 13, 2025 has the published fiscal estimate for SB 79. It says the Department of Housing and Community Development expects about $369,000 a year in ongoing General Fund costs. That money would cover technical assistance and handling complaints. Committee staff add possible review costs in the low hundreds of thousands a year. They also list unknown court costs and unknown local mandated costs. These are estimates, not spending.

Gaps: Only 1 of 17 analyses on file has fiscal text. The July 17, 2025 Assembly Appropriations analysis showed none.
